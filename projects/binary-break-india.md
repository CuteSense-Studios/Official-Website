# 🇮🇳 Binary Break India

> **"An advocacy, research, and data visualization archive dedicated to supporting diversity, inclusion, and underrepresented voices in tech across India."**

---

## 🕊️ Mission & Scope

Aligned with CuteSense Studios' core pillar of **Diversity and Advocacy**, **Binary Break India** is an ongoing research initiative and open-access data repository documenting the lived experiences, systemic hurdles, and community successes of minorities, non-binary individuals, and underrepresented developers in the Indian technology and creative sectors.

Through qualitative interviews, historical timelines, and open-source data visualization, Binary Break India advocates for safer workplaces, equitable hiring practices, and empowering creative networks.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights

- **📈 Interactive Data Visualizations:** Clean, accessible web charts tracking industry representation, pay equity metrics, and regional developer initiatives.
- **🎙️ Community Voices & Case Studies:** Oral histories and anonymous case studies detailing challenges and breakthroughs in indie game development and tech hubs.
- **📚 Educational Resource Directory:** Curated guides, mentorship programs, community grants, and legal aid references for marginalized creators in India.
- **🤝 Open Research Call:** Transparent data collection methodologies allowing researchers, activists, and organizers to review and contribute data points.
