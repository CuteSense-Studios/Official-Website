Our Business Model

Open by Design,
Sustainable by Community.

Quality art, openly created. We empower creative new voices through high-quality open-source tools and human-AI partnership—remaining independent, forever.

Lean & Transparent Operations

We operate without investor pressure. This means zero equity sold, allowing our decisions to be community-informed and founder-led.

  • Remote-First: No physical office, keeping overhead low.
  • Open Finances: Yearly public reports of anonymised income and expenses.
  • Diversity Stipends: 20% of net revenue is reserved for minority contributors.

Targeted Licensing Strategy

We match the right license to the right project to protect our community and our creations.

Project Type License Purpose
UI components, helper libraries MIT Maximum adoption; use in proprietary projects with credit.
Game engines, core frameworks Apache 2.0 Professional permissive license with patent protection.
Flagship tools (e.g., narrative editor) GNU GPL v3 Copyleft; ensures derivatives remain open source.
Games, stories, art assets CC BY-SA 4.0 Creative copyleft; share and adapt with attribution.
AI model weights (if any) OpenRAIL-M Responsible AI; restricts harmful use.

Diversified Revenue Streams

We generate revenue through convenience, services, and community support—never by locking core functionality behind paywalls.

Free & Open

All source code remains free forever. Local saving and community support via Discord/GitHub will never cost a dime.

Paid Convenience

Cloud sync, asset store credits, ad-free beta access, and premium high-quality open-source bundles (e.g., ready-to-run game binaries, curated asset packs).

Pro Services

Custom integration for indie studios, game dev workshops, and priority support SLAs.

Grants & Sponsorships

Funding via open-source grants (Sovereign Tech Fund, GitHub Accelerator) and community donations (GitHub Sponsors, Buy Me a Coffee, Open Collective, Ko-fi).

Merch & Community

Stickers, t-shirts, and plushies where 20% of profits directly fund diversity stipends.

AI & Copyright Policy

We use AI responsibly, strictly as a collaborator. Final creative decisions are always human-made, and transparency is our highest priority.

  • Always disclose AI assistance in code or metadata.
  • Use models trained on open-licensed or public domain data.
  • Strictly no training on user data without consent.
  • Public ATTRIBUTION.md or README.md logs in every repository.
